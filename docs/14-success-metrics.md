# 14. Success Metrics

---

## Metric Architecture: AARRR Framework for Sports Discovery

We measure success not by vanity pageviews, but by transaction enablement (clicks to registration) and user return frequency.

---

## 1. Acquisition Metrics (Supply & Demand Growth)

| Metric | Target (Month 1-3) | Target (Month 4-6) | Measurement Tool |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| **Monthly Active Users (MAU)** | 5,000 | 25,000 | Google Analytics |
| **New User Signup Rate** | N/A (Web is login-free) | 15% (when user profiles launch) | Custom Event |
| **Organic Search Share** | 30% of traffic | 65% of traffic | Google Search Console |
| **Total Events Listed** | 800+ ( Bangalore, Mumbai, Pune) | 2,500+ (Expansion cities) | DB Count |
| **Active Organizers** | 50 verified | 200 verified | DB Count |
| **Inbound Event Submissions** | 10/week | 50/week | Admin Log |

---

## 2. Activation Metrics (First Value Moment)

An athlete is "activated" when they perform a search, find a relevant event, and engage with it.

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|------------|--------|
| **Search Conversion Rate** | Users who search and click at least one event detail page | > 70% |
| **Registration Intent Click-Through Rate (CTR)** | Users who click "Register" on an event page | > 10% |
| **WhatsApp Share Rate** | Users who click "Share on WhatsApp" | > 5% |
| **Quick Compare Rate** | Users who compare 2+ events side-by-side | > 15% (when feature launches) |
| **Bounce Rate (City Landing Pages)** | Percentage of users leaving immediately | < 45% (target for high-intent search traffic) |

---

## 3. Retention Metrics (Habit & Utility Building)

Event discovery is a low-frequency behavior. We measure retention quarterly, corresponding to typical race planning cycles.

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|------------|--------|
| **30-Day Cohort Retention** | Users returning to the platform after 30 days | > 20% |
| **90-Day Cohort Retention** | Users returning after 90 days (next training block) | > 35% |
| **Newsletter/Digest Open Rate** | Open rate of weekly event roundups | > 40% |
| **Account Return Frequency** | Saved events updates / month | 2.5 visits/user/quarter |

---

## 4. Referral Metrics (Viral Growth Loops)

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|------------|--------|
| **Viral K-Factor (WhatsApp)** | Number of new users generated per WhatsApp share | > 0.15 |
| **Viral K-Factor (Instagram)** | Brand tags/reposts per organizer listing | > 0.05 |
| **Organizer Referral Count** | Organizers who register via a link shared by another organizer | 5% of new organizers |

---

## 5. Organizer Value Metrics (The B2B Retention Wedge)

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|------------|--------|
| **Claim Rate** | Percentage of seeded events claimed by their actual organizers | > 25% by Month 3 |
| **Listing Updates Frequency** | How often organizers update details, photos, or pricing | 1.5 updates/event |
| **Organizer NPS (Net Promoter Score)** | Feedback from organizers on lead quality and tool utility | > 50 |
| **Referral Traffic from Organizers** | Users arriving from organizer links or badges | 10% of total site traffic |

---

## 6. North Star Metric

### **Registration Intent Clicks (RIC)**
*The total number of clicks on external registration links redirected from our platform.*

* **Why this is the North Star**: It directly measures the value delivered to athletes (they found an event and decided to register) and the value delivered to organizers (they got high-intent referral traffic).
* **Target Month 3**: 1,500 RIC / month
* **Target Month 6**: 10,000 RIC / month
