# 1. Product Summary

## Product Name Options

| Name | Rationale | Risk |
|------|-----------|------|
| **SportScout** | Clear, action-oriented, sport + discovery | Generic, hard to trademark globally |
| **RaceBase** | Strong for endurance sports, implies a database | Narrows perception to racing only |
| **Khel** (खेल) | Hindi for "play/sport" — culturally resonant, short, memorable | May limit global expansion |
| **EventTrail** | Discovery + trail/adventure | Slightly generic |
| **GoAthletix** | Descriptive, clear user intent | Could be confused with wedding/corporate event planning |
| **SportPulse** | Energy, community, heartbeat | Common pattern, harder to stand out |

**Recommendation**: Use **"GoAthletix"** as the working name for now, but strongly consider rebranding to something more sport-specific before public launch. "GoAthletix" risks being confused with generic event management. A name like **"SportScout India"** or **"Khel"** would position better.

## Vision (One Paragraph)

Build the definitive sports event discovery platform for India — the place where every runner, cyclist, trekker, triathlete, and adventure athlete goes to find, compare, save, and plan their next event. Solve the fragmentation problem where events are scattered across Instagram stories, WhatsApp forwards, obscure websites, and club newsletters. Give organizers a zero-cost distribution channel that reaches the right athletes. Over time, evolve into the operating system for India's sports community: profiles, challenges, social feed, activity tracking, and AI coaching.

> **Vision expansion (2026-09):** the target end-state is a **sports-only super-app** on a discovery wedge — five sequenced pillars: **Discover → Host (sports-only event hosting) → Community + WhatsApp CRM (clubs, AroundU-style) → Marketplace (gear/nutrition/apparel) → Services (event travel + training centers)**. Market-validated and phased in **[`21-platform-expansion-vision.md`](21-platform-expansion-vision.md)**. Discovery remains the wedge; the other pillars are progressive surfaces, not simultaneous builds.

> **Scope broadening (2026-09):** beyond endurance/adventure, GoAthletix also covers **competitive & youth sports** — **skating, athletics (track & field), gymnastics, martial arts, team sports** — and their **training/academies**. It becomes a **family sports platform**: a parent can find a kids' skating competition *and* the academy that coaches for it in one place. New **age-category** (Kids / Sub-Junior / Junior / Youth / Open / Masters) and **training-center** dimensions are defined in **[`17-sports-taxonomy.md`](17-sports-taxonomy.md)**. Endurance still ships first as the launch wedge.

## Product Thesis (One Paragraph)

India's endurance and adventure sports market is growing 25-30% annually, but the discovery layer is broken. Athletes rely on word-of-mouth, Instagram scrolling, and WhatsApp forwards to find events. Organizers — especially small, local ones — have no affordable distribution channel beyond social media spam. There is no single platform that aggregates, normalizes, and makes searchable the thousands of running events, cycling brevets, treks, triathlons, and adventure races happening across India every year. This product fills that gap by being the "Google for sports events" — a discovery and planning utility that becomes indispensable before it tries to be a social network. The wedge is aggregation + search + save. The moat is data completeness, organizer relationships, and community trust.

## Why This Product Exists

1. **Fragmentation is the #1 pain**: There is no single source of truth for sports events in India. Events live on 50+ different platforms, Instagram pages, WhatsApp groups, and organizer websites with broken mobile experiences.

2. **Discovery is accidental, not intentional**: Athletes stumble upon events through friends, social media algorithms, or running club chatter. There is no way to systematically browse, filter, and compare events by sport, city, distance, difficulty, date, or price.

3. **Organizers waste time and money on distribution**: Small organizers spend disproportionate time creating WhatsApp forwards, Instagram reels, and DM campaigns. They need a platform that gives them distribution, not just a listing.

4. **No planning tool exists**: Athletes cannot plan their race calendar, compare two half-marathons, check reviews of past events, or set reminders for registration openings.

5. **The market is ready**: India had 1,500+ organized running events in 2024, 400+ cycling events, 200+ treks run by commercial operators, and hundreds of triathlons, adventure races, and fitness events. This number is growing fast, especially in Tier 2 cities.

6. **Existing solutions serve adjacent needs**: Strava is for tracking, not discovery. Eventbrite/Townscript are for ticketing, not sports-specific curation. BhaagIndia is listing-only with poor UX. No one owns the full discovery + planning + comparison experience for Indian athletes.
