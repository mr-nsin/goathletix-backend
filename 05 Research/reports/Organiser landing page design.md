# Rebuild the organiser page around every sport

> **Applied and updated, 2026-11-18.** The change list below was applied to the landing page. Two decisions came with it: each role has
> its own account (ADR-010), and the "Sign in" and "Sign up" buttons lead to one email-first organiser sign-in screen. Where this report
> says the organiser uses "the same Supabase identity as the participant side", read "a separate organiser account".

GoAthletix should ship /organisers as a self-contained organiser site: no consumer header or footer, a slim organiser header whose right side is a quiet "Sign in" text link and a filled "Sign up" button, and one email-first sign-in/sign-up card that works like IndiaRunning's organiser portal but is better staffed with proof and honesty. The IndiaRunning pattern, now recovered from its public JavaScript, is a single passwordless card: enter email, enter a 4-digit OTP, and new users add first name, last name, mobile (verified by a second OTP) and organisation name ([IndiaRunning organiser bundle](https://organiser.indiarunning.com/static/js/main.85bc350a.js)). The current page's engine (live console, chaptered reel, km-marker journey) is sound and matches the animation evidence, but it fails the brief in three ways: it shows only running, it is wrapped in consumer chrome, and it has no sign-in path. The fix is a sport switcher that morphs the hero console between eight reusable demo primitives, a support section built from channels and hours the team can actually staff, and a trust section built from verifiable facts instead of counts. Evidence is thinner than it looks. Every competitor page was read as text, so no rendered visual, header scroll behaviour or modal-versus-page choice was observed; Townscript has shut down and can no longer serve as a benchmark; Insider, BookMyShow and several global login pages were blocked; no source on India SMS OTP rules (DLT) was found; and IndiaRunning's "30%-50%" revenue-share line is unverified. The specs below mark which parts are evidence and which are recommendation.

## What IndiaRunning's portal actually does, and what it corrects

The earlier role-dashboard research reported that organiser.indiarunning.com returned only a page title and could not be rendered. That gap is now partly closed, with one caveat: the new notes still did not render the page. Copy and layout were read from the JS bundle's JSX strings and Tailwind classes, so text and structure are reliable while colours, hero art and spacing are not observed ([bundle](https://organiser.indiarunning.com/static/js/main.85bc350a.js)). The recovered picture is that the "login" URL is really a "Partner with us" landing page. A full-viewport hero carries a white sign-in card about 30rem wide floating on the right, with the headline "Organise remarkable events with ease." bottom-left. Below it sit three value cards, the line "Seamless Event Journey: Discover, Book, Train, Enroll, and Prepare Your Audience for Success", and five alternating feature blocks: List your Event, Manage Registration, Promote and Engage, Train with Fitpage, and Customer Support. There is no top navigation bar, no customer logo, no testimonial, no pricing and no event count; the only number is a self-reported "more than 1 lac visitors" a month, which is unverified. The footer is dark and minimal: Contact Us, Terms, Facebook and Instagram, and "Powered by Fitpage" ([bundle](https://organiser.indiarunning.com/static/js/main.85bc350a.js)).

The authentication flow corrects three earlier statements. First, sign-in and sign-up are one flow in one card, branching on whether the email is known (the card shows "Sign In/Sign Up"; a pending registration status switches it to a "Signup" form). Second, organiser login is email plus OTP with no password and no social login found in the code, whereas the earlier note had only the consumer privacy policy to go on, which mentions Google and Facebook sign-in and no OTP ([Privacy Policy](https://www.indiarunning.com/privacy-policy)). So the consumer and organiser doors differ in method as well as address. Third, the signup step asks for an organisation name and the bundle contains invite-by-email strings with expiry and "already registered" errors, which is public evidence that IndiaRunning's organiser accounts belong to an organisation with invited teammates. That supports the earlier recommendation for `organisations` with `memberships`, though it still does not show whether the organiser and participant credential stores are shared. The bundle also defines a `/desktop-only` route and a sidebar hidden below the `md` breakpoint, so authenticated organiser use on phones looks restricted; GoAthletix should treat the organiser entry as desktop-first but not block phones, since organisers often check in from a phone on race day.

Two earlier claims are carried forward only with caution. The T&C line that IndiaRunning "may retain roughly 30% to 50%" of event revenue came from a model-summarised fetch both note sets say to verify against the page, so it should not appear in any comparison copy ([T&Cs](https://www.indiarunning.com/tnc)). The same source is the origin of IndiaRunning's "1 campaign per week, audience limited to 6000" email limits visible in the bundle, which are strings in code rather than a published promise. They are useful as a signal of what organisers there get, not as figures to out-claim.

## The competitor landscape rewards a slim header, a quantified promise and a human path

Only the dedicated B2B pages in India follow a SaaS pattern. Hudle's /list-your-sports-venue page leads with "Digitize Your Sports Club at 0% Commission", shows about 18 institutional logos and eight named operator testimonials, and ends with phone, email and a "Request a Demo" block ([Hudle](https://hudle.in/list-your-sports-venue)). KonfHub publishes INR pricing with GST and support tiers (email 10am-7pm IST on the lower tier, phone on higher ones) and a weekly settlement rule ([KonfHub](https://konfhub.com); [settlements](https://kh30.konfhub.com/payments-settlements)). Most others bury the organiser entry: Playo's partner page is two sections and an "I'm Interested" button with no proof or pricing, MeraEvents puts organiser calls near the bottom of a consumer page, and District's only organiser link is in the footer ([Playo](https://playo.co/list-business); [MeraEvents](https://www.meraevents.com); [District](https://www.district.in)). Townscript, the former benchmark, now shows only a notice that its services are no longer operational ([Townscript](https://www.townscript.com/)). The shutdown is a genuine opening for Indian organisers, but the notes found no date or successor details, so it belongs in strategy, not in copy that names a competitor.

On headers, global standalone organiser sites converge on logo, three to five links, then a right cluster of a text "Sign in" or "Login", an optional outline "Contact sales" or "Book a demo", and a filled "Get started" ([Ticket Tailor](https://www.tickettailor.com); [Stripe](https://stripe.com); [Race Roster](https://raceroster.com/solutions/event-organizer)). RunSignup is the counter-example to avoid: it keeps "Find a Race" and "Create a Race" in one utility bar, mixing consumer and organiser jobs ([RunSignup](https://runsignup.com)). IndiaRunning itself has no organiser nav at all, which is a defensible minimum but loses the anchor links that let a sceptical organiser jump to fees or support. The reliable section skeleton, drawn from Eventbrite, Ticket Tailor, RunSignup and Shopify, is hero, proof, feature blocks, payments and fees, event types, testimonials, how it works in three steps, FAQ, closing CTA ([Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/organizer/); [Ticket Tailor](https://www.tickettailor.com)).

For the sign-in screen, the best verified single-card example is Luma: a "sign in or sign up" heading, "Continue with Email", then phone, Google and passkey alternatives, on a separate /signin page rather than a modal ([Luma sign-in](https://luma.com/signin)). Split screens with a testimonial or product panel are recommended by design guides but were not verified on any real platform ([Framer guide](https://framerwebsites.com/blog/split-screen-layout-design); [Lollypop](https://lollypop.design/blog/2025/october/saas-login-page-design/)). The honest reading is that IndiaRunning (floating card over a hero) and Luma (centred card) are the two observed patterns, and a split panel is a plausible third that nobody in the sample demonstrably uses.

## Showing every sport takes eight demo primitives and one switcher

The notes' strongest structural finding is that organiser needs differ along a few reusable axes, so eight small in-page animations can cover the whole sport list instead of one bespoke animation per sport. The sourced differences are real and specific. Triathlon India follows World Triathlon rules and bands ages (Youth 14-15, Junior 16-17, Senior 18-39, Masters 40+) as of 31 December, with the Youth band changing between 2020 and 2024 documents ([ITF Ranking Guidelines 2024](https://triathlonindia.com/triathlon/uploads/2024/03/ITF-Ranking-Guidelines-2024.pdf)). World Taekwondo uses age bands set by calendar year and, for cadets, height as well as weight ([WT Division of Participants](https://www.worldtaekwondo.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Division-of-Participants-by-Event.pdf); [WT cadet height](https://www.mastkd.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/WT-Competition-Rules-Interpretation-Cadet-Height-Categories.pdf)). Badminton India runs Senior, Junior U-19 and Sub-Junior U17, U15, U13 and U11 championships, with draws seeded from ranking results ([BAI structure via MBA](https://www.mbasso.in/webfiles/newsdocument/19442025034457Requisition_for_National_Championships__All_India_Major_Ranking_Tournaments_for_the_year_2025-26_(2).pdf)). HYROX has Open and Pro, Doubles (average age) and four-person Relay divisions ([HYROX India FAQ](https://hyrox.co.in/faq)). Road races need a publicly posted AIMS course certificate for timing certificates to be recognised ([Procam standards](https://tatamumbaimarathon.procam.in/race-categories/marathon/road-race-technical-standards-&-timing-recognition-guidelines)), and Uttarakhand trekking operators need registration and permits ([draft rules](https://uttarakhandtourism.gov.in/sites/default/files/document/type/Draft%20%20Trekking%20Rule%20%202022%20(Final).pdf)). Weight numbers are contested (AKFI kabaddi sources conflict; current WT tables unconfirmed), so no weight figure should appear in page copy.

No competitor read has a hero that changes per sport; RunSignup uses 16 icon cards that link out, Race Roster uses role cards, Hudle uses sport tiles ([RunSignup](https://runsignup.com); [Hudle](https://www.hudle.in/)). A pill row driving the hero console is therefore a differentiator, though its conversion effect is untested. Group the sports into about ten pills so the row does not carry fourteen: Running, Cycling, Multisport, Trek and Adventure, Swim, Racquet, Martial Arts, Team Leagues, HYROX and Fitness, Yoga and Camps. Selecting a pill changes four things together (hero line, console demo, three feature chips, use-case cards) and leaves the rest of the page static. The animation per sport follows from the primitives and is cheap because none needs a backend, a map or video.

| Sport group | Demo animation | What it shows the organiser | Build note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Running (road, trail) | Chip-time leaderboard rows re-sorting as finishers land, plus wave-start slot bar | Bibs, waves, timing import, certificates | Keep the existing live console as the default state |
| Cycling (gran fondo, brevet) | Elevation profile drawing left to right with checkpoint dots and control times | Route, cut-offs, checkpoints | SVG path with stroke-dashoffset |
| Triathlon, duathlon, aquathlon | Three-segment bar (swim, T1, bike, T2, run) filling with split times; age-group chip set to a 31 December cut-off | Legs, transitions, age bands | Reuse the km-marker journey as the segmented bar |
| Trek and adventure | Route line with elevation, batch seat counter filling, permit checklist ticking | Batches, permits, waivers | The notes' most convincing trek combination; no live maps |
| Swimming | Lane and heat grid assigning names; open-water wave variant | Heats, lanes, seeding by entry time | Grid cells with staggered fade |
| Racquet (badminton, tennis, table tennis, pickleball) | Bracket filling round by round; age-category pills U11 to U19 | Draws, seeding, age categories | Highest wow per line after the leaderboard |
| Martial arts (taekwondo, karate, judo) | Weight and age class picker that filters entrants, then a bracket | Weight classes, weigh-in, brackets | Show class names only, no kilogram figures |
| Team leagues (football, kabaddi, cricket) | Fixture ticker updating a league table | Fixtures, standings, rosters | Table rows reorder with layout transition |
| HYROX and functional fitness | Eight-station strip with Open, Pro, Doubles toggle changing the run and station sequence | Divisions and team classes | Toggle is the demo |
| Yoga, coaching, camps | Slot calendar with seats filling and a batch capacity badge | Batches, capacity, memberships | Calendar grid with counter |

Every demo needs the same safeguards. Label sample data inside the console ("Sample data. Names and times are made up", the line FounderDesk uses and the vault's inspiration note already adopts) ([FounderDesk](https://founderdesk.so)). Any auto-advancing demo or ticker lasting over five seconds needs a visible pause control to satisfy WCAG 2.2.2 ([W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html)). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by showing the final frame, and keep motion to transform and opacity ([web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/prefers-reduced-motion)). Hash each pill (#martial-arts) so paid campaigns and SEO variants can deep-link, render all panels in the DOM, and start animations only on visibility. Avoid parallax, zoom-on-scroll and full-screen motion, which are named vestibular triggers. Performance targets from Core Web Vitals are LCP at or under 2.5 s, INP at or under 200 ms and CLS at or under 0.1 ([web.dev Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals)); the hero should be an HTML console, not a video poster. Motion's `LazyMotion` with `domAnimation` keeps animation code near 5 kb plus 24 kb gzip against 43 kb for the full component ([Motion docs](https://motion.dev/docs/react-reduce-bundle-size)). The roughly 30 kb animation budget on the page is the notes' own target, not a published standard.

## Support and engagement tools must be sold as workflows, not as customers

Support promises in India lean on WhatsApp, phone and a named human. Wowsly's sports page leads with phone numbers, WhatsApp links for "Request a Demo" and a "Personal Assistance" comparison against ticket-only competitors ([Wowsly](https://wowsly.com/event/sports/)). IndiaRunning's organiser block promises that "our team dedicated to customer support remains a call or an email away", with a phone and email on its contact page and no published hours, WhatsApp or account manager ([Contact Us](https://www.indiarunning.com/contact-us)). Global platforms lead with a help centre and "book a call" and publish no response-time promises (RunSignup, Race Roster) ([RunSignup](https://runsignup.com/)). No retrieved page promised a numeric response time, so GoAthletix gives up nothing by omitting one. The credible launch promise is: channels the team can staff (WhatsApp, phone, email) with stated IST hours only if true, a named person who walks each organiser through setup and race day, and free help moving an existing Google Form or Excel list, which none of the retrieved pages advertised.

On engagement, the pattern that repeats across Race Roster, RunSignup, Race Entry and Eventbrite is email campaigns with templates, abandoned-registration recovery, promo and early-bird codes, referral rewards, and channel analytics. Race Roster documents a three-step abandoned-registration sequence at one hour, one day and two days ([Race Roster](https://raceroster.com/articles/race-to-success-with-abandoned-registration-campaigns)). In India, WhatsApp bulk updates for start times, results and ticket delivery, QR check-in, automated e-certificates and live leaderboards are the equivalents (Wowsly), and IndiaRunning's organiser app strings show campaign emails, reminders to unfinished registrants, confirmation SMS and email toggles, BIB and corral assignment, and results upload ([Wowsly](https://wowsly.com/event/sports/); [bundle](https://organiser.indiarunning.com/static/js/main.85bc350a.js)). The ranking of what organisers value most (recover incomplete registrations, one-click messaging to everyone, promo codes, channel attribution, clean results and certificates) is the notes' inference from emphasis, not a survey. No credible open-rate or recovery-rate benchmark was found, so quote none. WhatsApp Business API template and consent rules were not researched and must be checked before any WhatsApp claim is shipped.

The honest presentation for a platform with no customers follows from this. Show each tool as a labelled sample mock-up: a WhatsApp race-week composer with delivery states, an abandoned-registration timeline at one hour, one day and two days, a certificate and shareable stat card, a check-in scan. Mark each with one of three tags, "Live", "In pilot" or "Coming soon", matching the vault note that features not built get a visible "Coming soon". Replace counters and "Trusted by" strips with checkable facts: the payment provider by name and what it certifies, a published fee table, a refund and data-handling summary, the named team and their event backgrounds, a real founding-organiser programme with limited slots only if it exists, a roadmap or changelog, and a waitlist for early access. Avoid invented numbers, stock-photo testimonials, logos of events not served, and competitor benchmark percentages shown as GoAthletix results. Wowsly's counters ("3 Lakh+ tickets") carry no period or method, which is the credibility trap to avoid ([Wowsly](https://wowsly.com/event/sports/)). The notes did not review ASCI or other Indian advertising rules on testimonials and comparative claims, so legal sign-off is needed before any comparison copy.

## Concrete specification: structure, header, auth, copy

**Page requirements.** The route /organisers renders in its own layout group with no consumer header, footer, navigation or search. It carries a skip link, a single H1, a reserved-height announcement slot (to avoid layout shift from late banners) ([web.dev CLS](https://web.dev/articles/optimize-cls)), and `scroll-margin-top` equal to the header height. Footer is organiser-specific: Contact (WhatsApp, phone, email with hours), Terms, Privacy, Refund policy, fee table, status or changelog when they exist, social links, and a single "Back to GoAthletix.com" link in small type.

**Section order.**

| # | Section | Purpose | Honesty rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero with sport-switchable live console; primary "Sign up free", secondary "Watch the demo" or "Talk to us" | Promise plus proof in one view | "Sample data" label inside console |
| 2 | Trust strip of verifiable facts (payment partner, fee table link, refund policy, team, support hours) | Replace logos and counters | Only true statements |
| 3 | Sports switcher expanded into use-case cards (school meet, club league, corporate run, charity run, trek, academy camp) | Show breadth beyond running | Use cases are targets, not claims of customers |
| 4 | Chaptered demo reel: Create event, Open registration, Message, Check-in, Results | Tell the lifecycle story | Pause control, sample label |
| 5 | Feature bento grid with micro-demos | One idea per card | Live, In pilot or Coming soon tag |
| 6 | Support and engagement: four channel cards plus WhatsApp and recovery mock-ups | Reassure first-timers | Hours and channels staffed |
| 7 | Km-marker journey: three-step "from first draft to payout" | Narrative scroll device already built | Distances as steps, not claims |
| 8 | Fees and payouts summary (fee table, settlement rule, KYC gate) | Remove the biggest organiser doubt | Publish only agreed terms |
| 9 | FAQ with payout, KYC, refunds, free events, multiple sports, data ownership | Objection handling | Answers match legal terms |
| 10 | Closing CTA repeating Sign up and the human path | Funnel close | Same CTA wording as header |

The fee and payout section sits late because only Hudle ("0% Commission") and KonfHub (percentage plus GST) publish numbers on the page; a new platform that can publish a clear fee should publish it, since fee opacity is the common weakness of BookMyShow, District and Playo ([KonfHub](https://konfhub.com)). If the fee is not decided, say how it will be decided rather than leave the section empty.

**Header spec.** Left: GoAthletix wordmark with an "Organisers" label linking to the top. Centre: four to five anchors (Sports, How it works, Support, Pricing if a fee table exists, FAQ). Right: "Sign in" as a text link, an optional "Talk to us" outline button, and "Sign up" as the filled primary. This follows the Ticket Tailor, Stripe, Framer and Hudle pattern, and it keeps sign-in quiet and sign-up loud ([Ticket Tailor](https://www.tickettailor.com)). Behaviour (recommendations, since no source confirmed any header's scroll behaviour): `position: sticky` at a constant 64 px height; transparent over the hero, then a translucent surface with a 1 px border after about 16 px of scroll, triggered by an IntersectionObserver sentinel rather than a scroll listener; the hero CTA crossfades into the header only after it leaves view; hide-on-scroll is optional and never while focus is inside the header. On mobile, a hamburger opens a full-screen sheet with Sign in and Sign up pinned outside the sheet and 44 by 44 px touch targets.

**Sign-in/sign-up spec.** Use one unified screen at /organisers/sign-in (a page, not a modal, as Luma and IndiaRunning do; this also gives a stable URL for invites and password-free deep links). Layout: the form card on the left, a quiet product panel on the right reusing the hero console with a looping state, collapsing below about 1024 px; the split panel is a design-guide recommendation, not an observed competitor pattern, so keep the card usable on its own. Reduce the top bar to logo and "Back to site". Flow:

| Step | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Email | Heading "Sign in or sign up", one email field, "Continue" primary, "Continue with Google" secondary | Matches IndiaRunning's email-first card plus Luma's Google; add phone as a later option |
| 2. Code | "We sent a code to {email}", single `autocomplete="one-time-code"` input with `inputmode="numeric"`, 30 s resend countdown, "Change email" | web.dev recommends a single input, not `type=number` ([web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/sms-otp-form)); IndiaRunning uses four boxes and a 4-digit code, a weaker pattern since it ignores one-time-code autofill |
| 3. New organisers only | First name, last name, organisation or "I'm organising as an individual", mobile number | IndiaRunning also asks a verified mobile and organisation name; GoAthletix adds only what payouts and support need |
| 4. Intent and first run | "What do you organise?" sport chips, expected participants, then "Create your first event" | Carries the sport the visitor selected on the landing page |
| Invite variant | Email prefilled and locked, banner "You are invited by {organisation}", clear expiry and already-used errors | Mirrors IndiaRunning's invite strings |

Identity rule (updated 2026-11-18, ADR-010): the organiser account is its own account, separate from a participant account, with organiser intent flagged at signup (`account_type: organiser`), an organiser profile created as `draft`, and the organisation created or joined at step 3 ([earlier report, identity model](Role%20dashboards%20and%20onboarding.md)). Free events publish without KYC, paid events need KYC submitted, and payout waits for verified payee details, which should be stated on step 4 so organisers see the gate before they hit it. Email OTP delivery through Supabase Auth is a standard capability, but the notes did not verify it and no SMS OTP source for India exists. Phone OTP is therefore a decision for the backend and vendor team, who must check DLT sender and template registration, delivery reliability and a WhatsApp OTP option before it becomes the primary path. Until then, email OTP as the first factor and a phone number captured but verified later (or verified by WhatsApp) is the lower-risk launch. If phone OTP is used, WebOTP needs the last SMS line in the form `@domain #code` and works on Chrome for Android, with `autocomplete="one-time-code"` handling Safari ([MDN WebOTP](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebOTP_API)). Add a rate limit and a lockout message on repeated failed codes; IndiaRunning's only visible error is "Incorrect Otp". Use fixed form-container height between steps to avoid shift, and a 200 to 300 ms fade between steps.

**Copy guidance.** Lead with the organiser's job and the sport, not the platform: "Run your next event from one place" is stronger as a switchable line ("Run your next league from one place", "Fill your trek batches without spreadsheets"). Use plain verbs (Create, Open, Send, Check in, Publish), the Indian conventions of INR and "plus 18% GST" for fees, and "lakh" for large numbers only when a real number exists. Pair a primary "Sign up free" with a human secondary ("Talk to us on WhatsApp") because "free to start plus talk to us" is the dominant ticketing pairing ([Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/organizer/)). Prefer "Sample data" to "Demo" so no one thinks results are real. Never claim "24-hour go-live", response times, event counts, "trusted by" or "most loved" until each has been earned and documented. Avoid disparaging Townscript or others by name.

## Evidence gaps that limit these recommendations

The largest gap is visual. Nothing in this research saw a rendered page: IndiaRunning's portal was read from JavaScript strings, and every other site through text extraction, so modal versus split-screen, header scroll behaviour, colour, imagery and the real mobile experience are unobserved. The design patterns in the header and animation sections are therefore a synthesis of sourced elements and the notes' own recommendations. Blocked or unretrieved sources include Eventbrite sign-in, Stripe login, Dice, Let's Do This, Race Entry (partly), BookMyShow and Insider organiser pages, Skillboxes, and any HighApe or Fitpage organiser content. Townscript's closure is a single self-notice; the earlier report found one source that disagreed on whether it shut down, so its status should be re-checked before any strategic or copy use.

On authentication, no source covered India SMS OTP (DLT registration, template approval, delivery times), WhatsApp OTP, or WhatsApp Business API consent rules, so the recommendation to launch email-first is reasoned from IndiaRunning's design and from risk, not from a measured comparison. Sport domain coverage is partial: governing-body evidence exists for triathlon, taekwondo, badminton, kabaddi, HYROX, road races and Uttarakhand treks, and none for swimming, squash, skating, gymnastics, cricket, pickleball or table tennis, so those demos are recommendation only. Weight and age tables conflict across sources and must be confirmed against current rulebooks before any figure is shown. The India event mix (school meets, corporate runs, charity runs) has no quantitative support, and 80G, school-affiliation and corporate-procurement points were not researched. Finally, IndiaRunning's "30%-50%" retention, its "1 lac visitors" claim and Wowsly's counters are unverified and should not be compared against in public copy.

## Conclusion

The research shifts the question from "what should the page say" to "what can a new platform credibly prove." Competitors that look strongest (Hudle, KonfHub, RunSignup) win on a quantified promise, named peers and a human phone path, and GoAthletix can copy two of the three immediately: a published fee and payout rule, and a named, staffed human path. The third, peer proof, has to be earned, so the page should substitute labelled sample outputs and verifiable facts now and replace them with a real pilot event's real results as soon as one exists. The single most differentiating idea in the evidence is the sport-morphing console, because no sampled competitor does it and its eight primitives make breadth cheap.

IndiaRunning's recovered portal also changes how much to trust it as a model. Its sign-in card is a sound minimum (email, OTP, organisation, invites), but its landing content has no proof, no pricing and no support hours, and its 4-box OTP ignores autofill. GoAthletix can match the flow and beat the page.
