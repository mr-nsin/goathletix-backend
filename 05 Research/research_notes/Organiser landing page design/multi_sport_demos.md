# Multi-sport organiser landing page: per-sport organiser needs, animated demos, and competitor presentation patterns (India, 2026)

Note on method: about 14 tool calls. Competitor facts come from live page fetches; governing-body facts are limited to what search results surfaced. Sport-domain generalities that I could not cite are labelled as Inferences/recommendations, not facts.

## Q1. Which sport-specific organiser features should the landing page name, and which demos are most convincing and cheap to build as in-page animations?

### Takeaway
Organiser needs differ along a few reusable axes (categories and eligibility, brackets/fixtures, legs/stages, slots/batches, permits/safety, scoring/timing), so a handful of demo "primitives" can cover every sport. Cited evidence confirms the key differences for triathlon (age bands, transition/chip rules), taekwondo (age and weight/height classes), badminton (age-group draws, ranking-based seeding), kabaddi (age and weight caps), HYROX (divisions, average-age team classes), road races (AIMS course certificate) and treks (operator registration and permits).

### Cited Findings
- Triathlon India (ITF): follows World Triathlon Competition Rules for all categories it conducts in India — [ITF search result / triathlonindia.com](https://www.triathlonindia.com/events/important-race-rules)
- ITF 2024 ranking guidelines list age groups Youth (14-15), Junior (16-17), Senior (18-39), Masters (40+), with age set as of 31 December; a 2020 document used Youth 13-15, so bands change over time — [ITF Ranking Guidelines 2024](https://triathlonindia.com/triathlon/uploads/2024/03/ITF-Ranking-Guidelines-2024.pdf); [2020 Ranking Criteria](https://www.triathlonindia.com/triathlon/uploads/2020/07/Ranking-Criteria_Final-Version.pdf)
- ITF race rules cover checking the timing chip before leaving transition and not mounting the bike before the mount line; ITF has an age-fraud prevention page — [ITF race rules](https://www.triathlonindia.com/events/important-race-rules); [Age fraud](https://www.triathlonindia.com/age-fraud/)
- World Triathlon rules allow drafting in age-group events only at Sprint distance (per search summary, not verified in rulebook) — [ITF Race Rules PDF](https://www.triathlonindia.com/triathlon/uploads/2020/07/ITF-Race-Rules-Final.pdf)
- Taekwondo (WT): age bands Cadet 12-14, Junior 15-17, Senior 17+, determined by calendar year, not exact birthdate (per search summary) — [WT Division of Participants](https://www.worldtaekwondo.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Division-of-Participants-by-Event.pdf); [USATKD Kyorugi rules](https://assets.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blteb7d012fc7ebef7f/blt18952dcbc6109846/65fc44a3f2a2920e2ebf720f/2024_USATKD_Kyorugi_Rules_v2_3_21_24.pdf)
- Taekwondo cadet categories use height as well as weight; the WT Jan 2026 rulebook changes document discusses reduced senior weight categories (8 per the draft; status unconfirmed) — [WT cadet height interpretation](https://www.mastkd.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/WT-Competition-Rules-Interpretation-Cadet-Height-Categories.pdf); [WT Kyorugi changes 1 Jan 2026](https://www.taekwondobond.nl/wp-content/uploads/WT-Competition-Rules-and-Interpretations-Kyorugi-changes-made-as-of-January-1-2026.pdf)
- Badminton (BAI): national championships are split Senior, Junior U-19, Sub-Junior (U17, U15, U13, U11), Masters, plus All India Major Ranking tournaments — [MBA requisition citing BAI structure](https://www.mbasso.in/webfiles/newsdocument/19442025034457Requisition_for_National_Championships__All_India_Major_Ranking_Tournaments_for_the_year_2025-26_(2).pdf)
- BAI seeds junior selection draws from ranking-tournament results and has separate seeding circulars for Senior Nationals and Zonals; new players entering national U13-U19 events need a BAI player ID — [BAI 2026 draw format](https://badmintonindia.org/download/news/Draw%20Format%20for%20Final%20Selection%20Trials%20for%20the%20Badminton%20Asia%20Junior%20Championships%E2%80%93%202026.pdf); [BAI Competition Regulations](https://www.badmintonindia.org/download/regulations/BAI_Competition_Regulations-07.pdf)
- Kabaddi (AKFI): holds age-category nationals (35th Sub-Junior Boys & Girls, Nov 2025; 51st Junior Boys, Jan 2026); weight caps exist but sources conflict (senior men <85 kg, women <75 kg; junior boys 70 kg vs a reported rise to 75 kg) — [AKFI sub-junior circular](https://www.indiankabaddi.org/documents_2025/Circular_35th_Sub-Junior_National_Kabaddi_Championship-Boys_and_Girls.pdf); [AKFI junior circular](https://www.indiankabaddi.org/documents_2025/circular_51st_Junior_National_Kabaddi_Championship_2025_Boys.pdf); [AKFI notifications](https://www.indiankabaddi.org/important-notifications.html)
- HYROX: Solo Open/Pro by gender, Doubles (Women, Men, Mixed; Open/Pro), Relay of four (no Pro); same course, Pro uses heavier loads on five of eight stations; Doubles age = average of partners, Relay classed Under 40 / 40+; sources conflict on Pro age-group eligibility — [Red Bull guide](https://www.redbull.com/in-en/what-is-hyrox-guide); [HYROX India FAQ](https://hyrox.co.in/faq); [Hyranking divisions explainer](https://hyranking.com/learn/explainers/hyrox-divisions-explained-pro-open-doubles-and-relay-what-actually-changes)
- Road races: Procam races require a publicly posted AIMS/World Athletics course measurement certificate (certificate number, measurer grade A/B, validity) and may reject timing certificates without it — [Procam technical standards](https://tatamumbaimarathon.procam.in/race-categories/marathon/road-race-technical-standards-&-timing-recognition-guidelines)
- AFI has withheld recognition when course measurement was unclear even where AIMS recognition was claimed; India has few certified measurers — [Business Standard/PTI](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/afi-says-it-hasn-t-granted-permission-to-pune-int-marathon-117113001350_1.html); [Bengaluru workshop](https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/aims-world-athletics-conduct-course-measurement-workshop-bengaluru-209.htm)
- Treks (Uttarakhand): draft rules require operator registration (Rs 2,500 fee) via UTDB portal and online reporting of guides/porters/cooks to forest dept; permits issued at district level (SDM, forest, panchayat), 1-7 days processing; some protected areas require a registered local guide — [Uttarakhand draft trekking rules 2022](https://uttarakhandtourism.gov.in/sites/default/files/document/type/Draft%20%20Trekking%20Rule%20%202022%20(Final).pdf); [TourMyIndia permit guide](https://www.tourmyindia.com/treks/uttarakhand-trekking-permit.html); [Himalayan High](https://himalayanhigh.in/kedarkantha-trek-do-it-yourself-guide)
- Tournify (bracket/league tool) names: flexible formats (groups, knockouts, templates), team and player management, drag-and-drop scheduling, referee management, online registration with payment, live scores on site/app/slideshow — [Tournify](https://tournifyapp.com/)

### Inferences
- Recommended named features (all uncited domain judgement, to be validated with organisers): age/gender category rules with cut-off date (31 Dec for triathlon, calendar year for TKD); weight/height classes with weigh-in; seeded draws; pool-to-knockout and league fixtures with standings; multi-leg events with chip splits; batches/slots and capacity; permit and document checklist; team rosters; waiver and medical declaration; gear/kit and size (tee, belt); results and leaderboards; certificates.
- Demo primitives (cheapest first, each is CSS/JS state animation, no backend): (1) counter + progress bar (registrations, slots); (2) leaderboard rows re-sorting (bib/chip timing, HYROX, swimming heats); (3) bracket/draw filling (badminton, tennis, TT, pickleball, judo, karate); (4) league table with fixture ticker (football, cricket, kabaddi); (5) category/weight picker that filters (martial arts, age bands); (6) multi-leg segmented bar (triathlon, duathlon, aquathlon, HYROX stations, adventure races); (7) SVG route with elevation profile and drawing line (trek, trail, cycling brevet/gran fondo); (8) batch/slot calendar with seats filling (yoga, coaching camps, swimming lanes, gymnastics).
- Most convincing per effort: bracket fill, leaderboard re-sort, segmented multi-leg bar, route/elevation with counter. Weight-class picker is cheap and distinctive. Avoid anything needing real maps or video.
- Sport-to-demo mapping suggestion: running (chip leaderboard + wave start slots); cycling (elevation profile + checkpoint/control times for brevet); triathlon (3-leg bar with T1/T2); trekking (route + elevation + batch seats + permit checklist ticks); swimming (lane/heat grid + open-water wave); racquet (bracket/draw, age categories U11-U19, seeding); martial arts (belt + weight-class picker then bracket); skating/gymnastics (age category + judge score tally); team sports (fixtures + live table); HYROX (8 stations + run segments, Open/Pro/Doubles toggle); adventure (checkpoint tracker, waiver ticks); yoga/wellness (slot calendar, membership batch).

### Gaps
- No governing-body source was retrieved for TTFI, pickleball, squash, skating, gymnastics, swimming (SFI) or cricket rules, or for karate/judo India weight classes; mappings for those sports are recommendation only.
- Triathlon India distance table did not load (conduct-event page returned 404 on direct fetch).
- Official current WT and AKFI weight tables were not confirmed; do not quote numbers on the landing page without checking the current rulebook.
- AFI's own written sanction/permit criteria were not found.
- Official, final Uttarakhand group-size and insurance rules not found; the "one guide per five" figure is from an unofficial source.

## Q2. Which event types beyond races matter in India (school meets, club leagues, corporate runs, charity runs, trekking groups, academy camps)?

### Takeaway
Evidence for the India-specific mix is thin in cited form; what is documented is the age-category federation circuit (school/sub-junior/junior nationals in badminton and kabaddi), corporate team events as a named use case on RunSignup, and fundraising as a named organiser role on RaceRoster. The rest is recommendation.

### Cited Findings
- Age-category championships are core federation product in India (BAI U11-U19; AKFI sub-junior/junior nationals) — see Q1 sources.
- RunSignup lists Corporate Team Events, Kids Events, Virtual & Hybrid, Obstacle Course Races, Stair Climbs among event types — [RunSignup](https://runsignup.com)
- RaceRoster frames organisers by role: Event organizers, Timers, Fundraising coordinators, with fundraising and virtual events as products — [RaceRoster](https://www.raceroster.com/)
- Hudle presents venue/community/"Compete" with 40+ sports and has Corporate Solutions and Brand Solutions links, and testimonials about running a coaching programme — [Hudle](https://www.hudle.in/)
- Townscript (a major India events platform) shows a notice that its services have been discontinued, which leaves a visible gap in India event ticketing — [Townscript](https://www.townscript.com/)

### Inferences
- Recommended India use-case set for cards: school and inter-school meets; club leagues and academy tournaments; corporate runs and wellness challenges; charity/fundraising runs (80G receipts are a plausible India need, unverified); trekking groups and weekend trek operators; coaching/academy camps with batches; RWA/community events; virtual/hybrid challenges. Verify demand with interviews before featuring all.
- Townscript's shutdown (if the notice is current and permanent) is an acquisition angle for India organisers, but confirm status and avoid naming a competitor negatively.

### Gaps
- No quantitative India data on event mix by type found.
- No cited source on 80G, school-affiliation (SGFI/CBSE) or corporate wellness procurement; not researched.
- Playo and Eventbrite pages were not fetched; Playo's organiser positioning is unverified here.

## Q3. What is a good structure for a sport/use-case switcher where the hero console and copy change per sport?

### Takeaway
Competitors use icon-card grids (RunSignup), role-based cards (RaceRoster), and sport tiles linking out (Hudle, Tournify) rather than live tabs; none seen has a hero that morphs per sport, so a pill/tab switcher driving a hero demo would differentiate. Structure below is recommendation.

### Cited Findings
- RunSignup: a row of 16 circular icon cards under "Registrations for All Types of Events", most linking to use-case pages, plus a separate "Use Cases" dropdown by audience (Race Directors, Timers, Running Clubs) — [RunSignup](https://runsignup.com)
- RaceRoster: no sport breakdown; three role cards (Event organizers, Timers, Fundraising coordinators) and six product features (Registration, Fundraising, Virtual events, Onsite app, CRM, Timing tools) — [RaceRoster](https://www.raceroster.com/)
- Hudle: hero with Select Sport and Select City dropdowns, "Choose from 40+ Sports" tiles, feature cards (Community, Book Venue, Compete) — [Hudle](https://www.hudle.in/)
- Tournify: sport list of 12 sports with "See all sports", then feature blocks (formats, scheduling, referees, registration, live scores) — [Tournify](https://tournifyapp.com/)
- Strava (logged-out clubs page): "Top Sports" in navigation lists Running, Cycling, Walking; no pills/filters seen; clubs content not shown — [Strava](https://www.strava.com/clubs)
- Challonge (403) and Toornament (empty content) pages could not be read; Eventbrite not fetched.

### Inferences
- Recommended structure: sticky sport pill row (8-10 groups, scrollable on mobile): Running, Cycling, Multisport, Trek and Adventure, Swim, Racquet, Martial Arts, Team Leagues, Fitness/HYROX, Yoga and Camps. Group the 14 sports into these to avoid 14 pills.
- On select, change four things together: hero headline (one line naming the organiser job), console demo (one primitive), a 3-chip feature list (e.g. "Weight classes, weigh-in, brackets"), and a use-case card row (school, club, corporate). Keep the rest of the page static.
- Auto-rotate pills every ~6s until first user interaction, pause on hover/focus, respect prefers-reduced-motion (show static final frame), URL hash per sport (#martial-arts) for ads and SEO landing variants.
- Use semantic tablist roles for accessibility; render all panels in DOM for SEO but lazy-start animations on visibility.
- Add a second axis below the switcher ("What are you organising?": league, camp, race, trek, school meet) as cards, mirroring RunSignup's use-case idea.

### Gaps
- No data on switcher conversion performance for comparable B2B pages; this is untested.
- Could not verify Challonge/Toornament/Eventbrite/Playo/Strava Clubs marketing layout; recommend manual review in a browser.
